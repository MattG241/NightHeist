# Pacing: Novelty Cadence

Rule: something meaningfully new (a gadget, guard type, security system,
building type, objective type or job modifier) appears at least every **30–45
minutes** of first-playthrough time. The campaign target is **≥ 6 hours**
across about 25–35 heists of 12–20 minutes each.

Minute marks are **planned** until playtest logs (Phase 8) replace them with
measured values. The "Measured" column stays empty until then.

window goes without a new element.
